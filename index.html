🏫 2D School-Days Roleplay

Play as Stan, Kyle, Cartman, Kenny, Butters, Craig, Tweek, Wendy… or make your own New Kid with your own mom & dad (or just mom!), race, gender, hair & twitch!

Free-roam: Bus Stop → Playground → Hallway → Classroom → Cafeteria (lunch!) → Home. Everyone talks. Every hero has unique lines. Canon couples hold hands.

Fan parody. South-Park-style paper cutout art drawn live in code — big heads, beady eyes, mitten hands, flap mouths, waddle-walk. Movement: slow waddle + bounce like classic 2D kindergarten RPGs.

Desktop: keyboard required. Sound: none (respectfully quiet, like Kenny's hood).
