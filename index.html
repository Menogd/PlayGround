PAPER TOWN: School RP

A 2D fan-made school RP inspired by South Park's paper-cutout style.
All art & code here is original. Not affiliated with the show.

  • WASD / Arrows — walk (smooth, Kindergarten-style glide + wobble)
  • E / Space / Click — talk, pick up lunch, open doors
  • 1 / 2 — pick what YOU say (every playable kid says it differently)
  • 3 😈 — insult them! They'll get MAD. Some want to FIGHT (Kyle always beats Cartman — obvious. Cartman cries.)
  • 4 ✏️ — type ANYTHING yourself! Nice words = friends. Mean words = enemies. Try "fight", "sorry", their crush's name…
  • M — fast-travel map · L — lunch tray · Go to the Cafeteria at Lunch to eat
  • Teachers — Mr. Harris (class), Ms. Ellen + Principal Peters (hall), Coach Miller (playground). All talk differently!
  • Trouble system — swear in class, mouth off to a teacher, or fight → ⛔ DETENTION. Everyone except Cartman also gets GROUNDED — go home and face your parents! (Liane doesn't care. Lucky piggy.)
  • Main-4 meetups — as Stan, Kyle, Cartman or Kenny, pick 5 🤝 Hang out! to recruit the others. They follow you everywhere — into houses, into rooms!
  • Rooms — every house has Living Room + Kitchen + Bedroom (gold side-doors). Moms cook, dads lounge, the resident kid chills in their room. E the 🎮 console for a group hangout!
  • Follow the school day: Morning → Class → Lunch → Recess → After School. Talk to everyone!

Desktop + keyboard recommended. Audio: none (keep class quiet!).
